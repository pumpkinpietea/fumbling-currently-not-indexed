Two developments to follow in Southeast Asia's digital economy. The dates below distinguish today's reporting from a recent company announcement.

## Indonesia: journalism rights in the AI era

ANTARA reported on **10 October** that Indonesia's communications and digital ministry called for protecting journalism copyright at an ASEAN forum in Bali. The ministry's representative emphasized attribution, licensing and compensation when journalistic work is used by digital platforms or in AI development, and encouraged regional cooperation.

[Source: ANTARA, published 10 October 2026 at 11:00 WIB](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5779648/kemkomdigi-tekankan-pentingnya-pelindungan-hak-cipta-karya-jurnalistik)

## Vietnam: FPT puts data foundations before retail AI

In a release dated **9 October**, FPT described its CEO's presentation at a retail convention in Tokyo on **8 October**. Drawing on pharmacy chain FPT Long Chau, he stressed that businesses should own transformation outcomes and establish sound data foundations before expanding AI. The release also emphasized protecting personal information.
