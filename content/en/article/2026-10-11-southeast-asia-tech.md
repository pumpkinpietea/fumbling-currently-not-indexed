## Indonesia: Telkom outlines foundations for business AI adoption

In an October 9 announcement, Telkom Indonesia described its AI strategy presented at a DTIIF-related event in Jakarta on October 8. It outlined an integrated approach spanning connectivity, cloud, computing, AI models and business applications, alongside data readiness, talent development and collaboration across sectors.

[Source: Telkom Indonesia · 2026-10-09 · Company announcement](https://www.telkom.co.id/sites/berita/id_ID/news/telkom-ungkap-strategi-percepat-adopsi-ai-nasional-di-road-to-dtiif-2026-3974)

## Vietnam: Data centre growth puts pressure on power infrastructure

Viet Nam News reported on October 10 that Vietnam’s expanding data centre market faces energy and infrastructure constraints. Data centre power capacity is projected to rise from about 580 MW in 2025 to nearly 980 MW by 2030. ABB Vietnam highlighted pressure on transmission and distribution systems as well as electricity supply.

[Source: Viet Nam News · 2026-10-10 11:54 WIB](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1801684/data-centre-market-faces-energy-challenges.html)

## Vietnam: Cross-border e-commerce requires stronger sales and logistics

An October 10 Viet Nam News report highlighted the need for sustained investment in brands, sales operations and logistics to expand Vietnamese online exports. Citing SHS Securities, it identified high logistics costs, long international delivery times and limited bonded-warehouse infrastructure as barriers. Inventory management and customer service also become more demanding as orders grow.

[Source: Viet Nam News · 2026-10-10 08:49 WIB](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1801525/businesses-need-to-expand-share-of-online-export-market.html)

## Japanese research: Vietnamese shoppers move between social media, e-commerce and stores

An October 8 JETRO report described Vietnamese consumers discovering products through social media and video, comparing prices and reviews online, and checking products in physical stores. Drawing partly on July 2026 interviews with Japanese businesses operating in Vietnam, it highlighted the importance of connecting online and in-store customer interactions and explaining product quality and warranties.

[Source: JETRO · 2026-10-08 · Research report](https://www.jetro.go.jp/biz/areareports/special/2026/1001/ec6579792c8494fd.html)

## China–ASEAN: Summit highlights a collection of 31 AI cooperation projects

An October 10 announcement from the China–ASEAN Business and Investment Summit (CABIS) reviewed a forum held in Nanning on September 17 and introduced a collection of China–ASEAN AI cooperation cases. The collection covers 31 projects across eight sectors, including manufacturing, logistics, agriculture and healthcare. Participants from Indonesia and other countries discussed industrial AI deployment and cross-border coordination.

[Source: CABIS · 2026-10-10 · Organiser announcement (event: 2026-09-17)](https://www.cabis.gov.cn/html/en/html/2026/CABISHighlights_1010/722.html)
