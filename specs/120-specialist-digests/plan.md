# Specialist digest design

Issue: https://github.com/pumpkinpietea/fumbling-currently-not-indexed/issues/120

Keep specialist collection separate from the Indonesia/SEA daily RSS and edition contracts. First delivery is a repeatable operator collector and reviewed October17 publication; no implicit cloud scheduling or extra daily model calls. Reuse secure feed transport, saxes/parse5, existing article/news-directory markup and main-only Hosting delivery. No new runtime dependency.

Registry entries identify stream, publisher country/type, format, exact URL/allowed article paths, access review and title/excerpt scope. Support RSS2, namespace-aware RDF/Atom and narrowly scoped dated link lists on official pages. Distinguish original published dates and updated dates; ambiguous dates fail, date-only values preserve precision rather than fabricate a time. Per-source errors stay visible; usable sources survive independent failures. Bound one request/source, zero retries, four concurrent, ten seconds/request, one MiB/response, at most sixteen sources and500 retained records. Reports are create-only outside Git.

Public Hacker News search supplies bounded private social discovery, eight watchlists across both streams, at most20 hits/query. Preserve exact points/comments, query, retrieval time and original URLs; no personal usernames/comment text/model evidence. Show matched officially accepted originals as private ranking hints only; no URL dereference from social results. Official GitHub release streams are vendor publication channels, not proof of social popularity. Do not scrape login-protected X/Weibo/LinkedIn, buy APIs, bypass access challenges or send social messages. Vendor release URLs are announcement references; supplied account-post URLs require manual verification.

A reviewed candidate references exact evidence IDs/hashes and quotes; localized concise factual notes have consistent numbers and cited source dates. Optional cyber trend observations reference selected notes and cannot turn a vulnerability into a confirmed breach or publisher geography into attack origin. Independent review and operator inspection precede publication. Source coverage counts measure selected evidence only. Separate articles use distinct slugs/translation keys and actual publication time. Existing date-specific daily state and content remain unchanged.

Cost: local collection uses no GCP service or paid API; initial manual generation/review uses the existing authorized plan, at most three calls for this operator edition (one generation stopped at its stream bound, one rejected review, one corrected-candidate review), zero paid fallback. No recurring infrastructure increase or new billing resource. Existing total GCP spend remains unmeasured; USD10/month is the total cap. Scheduled specialist collection/publication needs a separately evidenced rollout within shared model/runtime budgets and is not activated here.
