## Requirements and acceptance

Expand active previous-Jakarta-day Southeast Asia IT/e-commerce evidence beyond ANTARA. Add JICA official announcement titles, Viet Nam News official economy/industries RSS excerpts and Global Voices East Asia/technology RSS excerpts. Preserve original dates, publisher identity and direct links. Global Voices requires author, CC BY 3.0 link and an adaptation indication. No bodies or images are fetched.

Source-specific feed/host/path checks apply both before inference and to publication bundles. Candidate-only or restricted sources must never enter inference. Publisher-balanced selection uses at most three sources and two model calls; multilingual technology and Southeast Asia relevance are checked before selection and again by skeptical review. Geography alone never proves viewpoint or corroboration. All selected feeds must succeed; partial collection does not publish. Existing locks, freshness, numeric/quote/hash checks and main-only delivery remain in force.

Verify malformed attribution, source spoofing, wrong-host feed records, unsupported encodings, old records, insufficient evidence, fair selection and mixed-publisher bundle rendering. Record actual official-feed connectivity and a bounded private generation/review check, then passing CI and immutable runtime deployment. No new service or paid fallback; retain 600 seconds, one task and zero retries.
